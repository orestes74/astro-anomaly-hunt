Is there a star no one has ever seen, hiding in my own photos?

Nearly a year of amateur astrophotography — about ten thousand frames, 89 sessions — re-analyzed for anything that isn't on the map of the sky: moving objects (asteroids, comets), changing objects (novae, supernovae, variable stars), and faint dust and gas.

RESULTS
- 26 trails of known asteroids and comets re-detected (275 Sapientia, 1008 La Paz, 1675 Simonida, comet C/2025 R3 PANSTARRS, ...)
- 33 known variable stars re-detected (TT Ori, V808 Ori, FN Cep, ...)
- Faint-asteroid search (GPU shift-and-stack) reached about magnitude 18: no new objects
- New stars / novae / supernovae: none
- One unexplained flash: 2026-04-17 18:01 UTC, toward M51. No match among 8,916 public or 451 classified (amateur-tracked) satellites.
  If you know what it might be, please tell me in the comments!

FREE & OPEN SOURCE — try it on your own photos
The whole pipeline is open on GitHub: drop new images into your raw folder, run one command, and it sorts sessions, stacks, hunts for asteroids, compares brightness, checks dust, and writes a report with a "check by eye" list.
https://github.com/orestes74/astro-anomaly-hunt
